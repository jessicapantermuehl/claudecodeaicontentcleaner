Your environment is part of your health, too.

Most of us think about wellness in terms of what we eat, how we exercise, how we sleep, and what we put on our skin.

We rarely think about the ordinary environment surrounding all of it.

But we spend the vast majority of our lives indoors, and the Environmental Protection Agency notes that concentrations of some pollutants are often higher indoors than outdoors.

That doesn’t mean your home is “toxic.” And it definitely doesn’t mean you need to throw everything away.

It means your environment is one more foundation you can make more supportive, often through surprisingly small changes.

This one’s for you if…

  • You’ve already paid attention to food and personal-care products and are wondering what else is actually worth considering
  • You keep hearing words like “toxins,” “VOCs,” and “endocrine disruptors” and want someone to explain what deserves your attention without scaring you
  • You want a healthier home but have no interest in throwing away everything you own
  • You rent, live on a budget, or simply need recommendations that work in real life
  • You’d love a straightforward checklist instead of another rabbit hole to research

You just need to know what’s worth your attention and what isn’t. That’s what this course is for.
