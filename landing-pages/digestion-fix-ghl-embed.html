If your digestion feels unpredictable, the answer isn’t always another food to cut out.

Maybe you’ve tried eating “cleaner.”

Maybe you’ve removed foods, added probiotics, taken digestive enzymes, or reached for something when the bloating or reflux hits.

And maybe some of it helped.

But if you still don’t really understand why your digestion gets thrown off in the first place, it can start to feel like an endless game of trial and error.

That’s the piece I want to help you understand.

Your digestive system is doing a lot more than simply processing the meal you just ate. It has to break food down, absorb the nutrients inside it, and move everything through a whole series of steps that depend on different parts of your digestive system doing their job.

And digestion starts earlier than most people realize. Your brain and nervous system are involved before food ever reaches your stomach.

So when something feels off, the question isn’t always just:

“What food caused this?”

Sometimes the more useful question is:

“Where in the digestive process could my body use more support?”

That’s what The Digestion Fix is designed to help you understand.

  • You eat pretty well, but your digestion still doesn’t seem as happy as you’d expect
  • You’ve removed foods, tried probiotics or digestive enzymes, or experimented with other gut-health fixes and still feel like you’re guessing
  • You keep hearing about stomach acid, “rest and digest,” the microbiome, food sensitivities, digestive enzymes, and bile — but no one has ever connected the dots for you
  • You want to understand what may be contributing to your symptoms instead of endlessly chasing them one at a time

You don’t need another list of foods to avoid. You need to understand what your digestion is trying to do — and where it may need support.

  1. 01

    First, understand what’s actually happening

    Before we talk about what to change, I want you to understand what your digestive system is trying to accomplish in the first place.

    We’ll follow food through the digestive process and look at two of its most important jobs: breaking food down and absorbing the nutrients inside it.

    You’ll see how the different parts of your digestive system work together, where things can start to go off track, and why digestive symptoms don’t always tell you the whole story.

    We’ll also talk about some of the common ways people manage digestive symptoms and why simply quieting a symptom doesn’t necessarily help you understand what may be happening underneath it.

  2. 02

    Digestion starts before the food reaches your stomach

    This is the part most people never learn.

    Digestion actually begins in your brain.

    Seeing, smelling, and anticipating food begins preparing your digestive system before you ever take a bite. Your nervous system also plays an important role in the digestive process.

    We’ll talk about:

    • what “rest and digest” actually means
    • how stress can affect the digestive process
    • simple ways to help your body shift into a more digestion-supportive state before meals
    • why slowing down and chewing well matter more than they get credit for
    • how sleep and ongoing stress can influence digestive health over time

    None of this requires turning every meal into a wellness ritual.

    It’s about understanding what helps your body do its job well.

  3. 03

    Then we move deeper into the digestive process

    Once food leaves your mouth, there’s a whole chain of events that has to happen.

    Your stomach, pancreas, liver, gallbladder, and small intestine each play a role in breaking food down and making its nutrients available to your body.

    Inside the program, we’ll look at what each of those organs is doing and some of the ways you can support the process through everyday choices.

    We’ll also talk about:

    • foods that can support digestion
    • why different people can respond differently to the same foods
    • food sensitivities and how elimination-style approaches are sometimes used to identify patterns
    • how to read ingredient lists and nutrition labels with a more informed eye

    The goal isn’t to hand you a giant list of “good” and “bad” foods.

    It’s to help you make more sense of how your body responds.

  4. 04

    Your microbiome + your Digestion Fix plan

    We’ll also talk about your microbiome — the community of microorganisms living inside your digestive tract — and how it fits into the bigger digestive picture.

    Then we’ll bring everything together.

    You’ll use what you’ve learned, along with your Symptom Assessment + Tracking Workbook, to look for patterns and identify where it makes the most sense to focus first.

    Because by this point, you’re not just collecting more gut-health information.

    You actually understand how the pieces connect.

This isn’t about finding a “perfect” diet.

I don’t want you leaving this program with an even longer list of rules. I want you to leave understanding your digestive system better than you did when you came in.

Because the goal isn’t to become afraid of food. It’s to feel less confused by your own digestion.
